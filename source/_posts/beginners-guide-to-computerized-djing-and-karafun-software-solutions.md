However, we regularly hear from many professional KJs that are still using those circular plastic CDG thingy's (we'd like you to be able to use them as drink coasters), but with many looking to finally make the leap to a digital karaoke rig. 

**Hosting Karaoke** and "karaoke night" are**as popular as ever**, which I think can be attributed to, at least in part, due to the selection of singing contest shows on TV.
